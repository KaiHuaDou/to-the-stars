# Frigate

A small, lightweight ship deployed in large numbers to defend the rest of the fleet and deploy drones, mines, and flak. Used by both [Human](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Human) and [Cephalopod](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod) [fleets](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Fleets).

## Human

Typically crewed by a captain, an engineer, a weapons officer, a medical specialist, a pilot, and the ship [AI](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/AI) themselves. Very limited space, but equipped with its own FTL core. Often put in the front of fleet formations as a screening line. In the Battle of Orpheus, hundreds of thousands were deployed, and thousands were lost.

These ships have a notable variant, the Stealth Frigate, which is used by MagOps teams.

## Cephalopod

Holds similar duties compared to its [Human](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Human) counterparts, with the addition of operating as a light anti-personnel platform. Like all [Cephalopod](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod) ships, they benefit from their superior forcefield technology.
