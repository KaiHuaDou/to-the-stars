# Subcommittee on Black Operations

Also referred to as the "Secret Executive Subcommittee on Black Operations", this is a secret branch of the [Leadership Committee](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Leadership_Committee) of the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY). Little is known of its function or responsibilities, except that it was responsible for regulating and approving all uses of memory manipulation magic. However, this formal requirement was only customarily enforced for large-scale Reformatting.\[1]
