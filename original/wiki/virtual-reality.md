# Virtual reality

Virtual reality, fully immersive, is available in the modern day (\~2460) but very expensive, a deliberate policy decision by [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance) to prevent overuse. True high fidelity [VR](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/VR) generally involves plugging a cord directly into a port at the back of the neck, often with a place to lie down.
