# Heliopolis

The capital city of Apollo in the [Kepler-37 System](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kepler-37_System), where [Patricia von Rohr](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Patricia_von_Rohr), [Kishida Maki](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kishida_Maki), and [Shirou Asaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shirou_Asaka) fight during the Battle of Orpheus. Largely destroyed on the surface, but held by humans with production shifted underground.
