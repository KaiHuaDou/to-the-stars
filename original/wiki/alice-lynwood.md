# Alice Lynwood

Not much is known about **Alice**, but she was part of the same training cohort as [Patricia](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Patricia_von_Rohr) and [Asaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shirou_Asaka), both she became good friends and in the latter case lovers with.

In her abilities she was not particularly strong and was soon eclipsed in rank by Asaka. She planned to propose to Asaka at the end of their current shift, but she died before then defending a colony under the acting commander, Asaka herself, who was forced to send her lover in a deadly mission without so much as a word of goodbye.
