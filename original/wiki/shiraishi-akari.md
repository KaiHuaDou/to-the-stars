**Shiraishi Akari** was member of the original team that held [Kuroi Nana](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nana) and [Kuroi Nakase](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nakase). Her theme color is green.

Akari was a college undergraduate at the time, and decidedly composed and level-headed compared to [Takukatsu Yuka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Takukatsu_Yuka). She is usually deferential to [Nakajima Emiri](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Nakajima_Emiri), but is not afraid to put Yuka in her place or criticize Emiri when necessary, and is fairly harsh when she does. Otherwise, she does not stand out very much.
