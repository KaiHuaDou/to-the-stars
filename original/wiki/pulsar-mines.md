# Pulsar mines

A mysterious portion of the [Cephalopod](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod) supply chain, in which the space-time surrounding a pulsar is somehow mined for use in their Paradox Engines and [Blink Cannons](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Blink_Cannon). Humanity was able to identify one mine some distance into [Ceph](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ceph) space as a potential military target, hoping stave off further incursions into human space in the medium term.
