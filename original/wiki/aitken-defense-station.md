# Aitken Defense Station

**Aitken Defense Stationn** is a military defense station on the far side of the moon, presumably intended for the defense of Earth. It is larger than [Armstrong Military Defense Station](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Armstrong_Military_Defense_Station) and contains missile batteries, forcefields, heavily fortified bunkers, mining facilities, and other unknown facilities.

It is likely located in or around [Aitken crater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aitken_\(crater\)).
