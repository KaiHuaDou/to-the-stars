# Characters

Below is an exhaustive list of characters found in [To the Stars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/To_the_Stars). To arbitrarily and unfairly categorize:

- "Main Characters" are those whose viewpoints are consistently shown
- "Secondary Characters" are those who also play major roles
- "Tertiary Characters" are those who appear throughout the story
- "Quarternary Characters" are those who only play minor roles
- "Quinary Characters" are those who are only mentioned rarely

Names only mentioned once or twice in passing are left out entirely, unless otherwise notable.
