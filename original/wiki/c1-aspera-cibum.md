# C1 Aspera Cibum

> Beef bourguignon served as the centerpiece of the meal, flanked by mashed potatoes and a salad of local vegetables, including the fuzzy red lettuce locals always tried to scare Earthers with.
>
> - —[Chapter 33](http://www.fanfiction.net/s/7406866/33/To-the-Stars)

The scientific name [Asami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Asami) gives for a variant of the "fuzzy red lettuce" native to Nova Terra. It was genetically engineered to be edible by humans, and is expensive on [Earth](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Earth).
