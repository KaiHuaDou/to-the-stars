# SHERMAN Cannon

The SHERMAN (**S**uper **H**eavy **E**xotic-shielded **R**elativistic **MA**ss driving **N**ucleus) cannon is the primary gun of the Human [battlecruisers](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Battlecruiser). Named after a renowned [general of the 19th century](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Tecumseh_Sherman), it is the largest payload weapon of the human Navy, with a kinetic energy force equal to 9.6 megatons of TNT. Its use as the main gun of Human [battlecruisers](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Battlecruiser) is due to its unique ability. When fired, the projectile's exotic matter core produces anomalous gravitational fields that disrupt both [FTL](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FTL) and sub-light speed propulsion, fields which can be adjusted by the weapon operator before firing.
