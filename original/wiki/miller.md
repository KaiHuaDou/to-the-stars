**Fleet Admiral Miller** is a member of the [General Staff](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/General_Staff).

He is shown to oppose [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami)'s appointment to the position of Commander of the Euphratic Sector. He seems to be prejudiced against Magical Girls, unwilling to accept the position and respect [Magical Girls](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) gained in the twenty years since the Battle of Epsilon Eridani.
