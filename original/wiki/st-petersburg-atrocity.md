# St. Petersburg Atrocity

The **St. Petersburg Atrocity** occurred in 2163, and is considered the proximate cause of the [Unification Wars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Unification_Wars), analogous to the assassination of Franz-Ferdinand and the First World War.

> The local hyperclasses had resolved to do the unfathomable: annihilate an entire segment of the city’s population for anti-governmental behavior. While the world was shocked by this, the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) was further shocked by a subsidiary revelation–that a substantial number of newly contracted girls were dead in the event, not from an accident as originally believed, but due to the deliberate betrayal of members of the MSY’s [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA)-sympathizing faction.

As a result, the MSY firmly sided with the [United Front](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/United_Front).
