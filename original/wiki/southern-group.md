# Southern Group

A group of [Mahou Shoujo](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mahou_Shoujo) initially consisting of [Mikuni Oriko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mikuni_Oriko) and [Kure Kirika](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kure_Kirika), and later joined by [Miroko Mikuru](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Miroko_Mikuru), [Hinata Aina](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Hinata_Aina), and even later, [Chitose Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chitose_Yuma). One of the early enemies of the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY)'s founders, the Southern Group's activities were characterized by hunting down both demons and magical girls alike, and tormenting [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami) and [Sakura Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sakura_Kyouko). The group collapsed after [Chitose Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chitose_Yuma) betrayed and destroyed them through the use of super-saturated grief cubes that spawned an overwhelming horde of demons.
