# Blink Technology

[Squid](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Squid) technology that allows for apparent teleportation. Used in Blink Drives to let their ships teleport into and around [Human](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Human) space, and used in [Blink Cannons](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Blink_Cannon) for the [Blink Bombardment Cannon](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Blink_Bombardment_Cannon) which teleports weaponry directly into enemy fleets.

The technology uses contained space-time fragments, such as those from the [pulsar mines](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Pulsar_mines). Such fragments are used to resolve the massive energy requirements needed to create and maintain wormholes used for teleportation.

Additionally, the [Ceph](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ceph) have developed *blink interdictors*, which can counter/prevent Blink Drives from working. They also disable [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko)'s ability to teleport. It is inferred that these have been developed in case Humans have developed Blink Drives of their own.
