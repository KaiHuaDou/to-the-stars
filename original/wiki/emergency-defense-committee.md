# Emergency Defense Committee

Also known as the EDC. Essentially seized control of the collapsing national governments of the [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF) mid-way through the [Unification Wars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Unification_Wars), and declared itself the government of all humanity upon their conclusion. Removed itself in favor of its successor government, [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance).

Notably, [Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Yuma) was a member.
