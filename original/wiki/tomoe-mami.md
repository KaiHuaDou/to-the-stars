**Tomoe Mami** (巴 マミ *Tomoe Mami*) is a major military leader for Humanity and current [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) Military Liason, a position proceeded by her centuries of leadership in MSY. She acts as a mentor figure to [Shizuki Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko), though the motives for this aren't kindness alone.

## Story

Mami is first introduced while searching for potential magical girls in Mitakihara City. She rescues Ryouko from a demon attack, and is the one to push her into a contract. Mami also provides commentary while watching the Akemi (movie).

Mami contributed greatly to planning a (failed) MagOps attack against a Wormhole Stabilizer in the [Kepler-37 System](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kepler-37_System). She, along with [Chitose Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chitose_Yuma) and [Sakura Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sakura_Kyouko), are also the ones investigating mysterious administrative problems (the grief cube shortages and the disappearing [Magical Girls](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl)) as well as the enigma surrounding Ryouko (the attempt on Ryouko's life, Ryouko's friend [Simona del Mago](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Simona_del_Mago), the suspicious [Joanne Valentin](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Joanne_Valentin), and the surprise attack on the *HSS Pierre-Simon Laplace*).

## Background

### Before Reset

Her parents died in a car crash when she was only a child, Mami herself only surviving via a wish to [Kyubey](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyubey). As such, she was forced to learn to fend for herself. She survived solo demon hunts for over a year, taught herself how to form muskets, and mentored Kyouko, [Akemi Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akemi_Homura), and [Miki Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Miki_Sayaka) before the point at which Homura gains the memories of erased timelines.

### Ten Year Gap

Before the formation of the MSY, Mami's money was the primary funding that the Four had, and meetings amongst the Four or with other groups were based at her apartment(s), with her serving refreshments.

She nursed an uncharacteristically heavy grudge with [Mikuni Oriko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mikuni_Oriko), who had gone out of her way to harass her, her territory, and her home. When Oriko died, Mami's only thought was, "I never thought I'd see the day she would go down... I never got to get my revenge."

### MSY

One of her aliases during this time was "Tomatsu Mai". It is known that she rotated through many roles, including the title of Chief Diplomat.

Mami was the first Director of the [Soul Guard](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Soul_Guard), and served until a mission to eliminate an as of yet unnamed girl finished eating away at her guilt.

### After Reveal

After the Battle of Epsilon Eridani, Mami became a Field Marshal and serves on the [General Staff](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/General_Staff). Her biggest accomplishment to date is the Saharan Raid, where she covered Field Marshal [Erwynmark](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Erwynmark)'s retreat (following a successful assault).

Her flagship is the battlecruiser *HSS Georgy Konstantinovich Zhukov*.

### Reformat

[Akiyama Akari](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akiyama_Akari) was a [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) and girlfriend to Tomoe Mami. She exhausted her soul gem after the plane they were in was struck down by a bomb planted by a stray group of North Korean Magical Girls. The trauma of witnessing the decaying of Akari's soul gem caused Mami to reformat herself, which would naturally degrade after a few centuries.

Centuries later, the reformat was reversed by Homura, giving evidence that Homura was alive.

## Character

Mami works hard to project an image of matronly responsibility and has always been the "respectable older sister" figure, as well as the diplomat, of the [Mitakihara Four](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mitakihara_Four). During the Cold War with [SMC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/SMC), Mami was the only one of the elder Leadership members to take on a conciliatory stance amongst a sea of poker faces.

Mami is an excellent cook, and still prefers to use fresh produce instead of synthesized ones.

## [TacComp](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/TacComp)

Mami is equipped with a v2 TacComp named Machina, which acts as her close adviser, secretary, and personal processing core. Mami suspects Machina might be fond of Field Marshal Erwynmark, which has "some possibly disturbing implications".

In a vision, Mami learns that Machina is sentient (ref: [Chapter 51](http://www.fanfiction.net/s/7406866/51/To-the-Stars)). Machina portrays herself as very young (smaller than Yuma), in contrast to [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko)'s TacComp [Clarisse](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Clarisse) who acts about the same age as Ryouko. It breathed new life into Mami's life. Machina and Clarisse are the two TacComps so far who have witnessed a vision.
