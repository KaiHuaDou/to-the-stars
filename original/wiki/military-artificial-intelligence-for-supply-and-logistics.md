# Military Artificial Intelligence for Supply and Logistics

More commonly known as MAISL. Runs supply and logistics for the Human military, including aspects of grief cube distribution.

Notably mentioned in a [Chapter 1](http://www.fanfiction.net/s/7406866/1/To-the-Stars) opening snippet as recommending the cutting of [Alloc](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Alloc) distribution to help increase military production.
