# Contact War

The war with the [Cephalopods](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod). Began in 2440 with their attack on the human colony of Aurora and shortly after the end of the masquerade at the battle of New Athens. By the beginning of [To the Stars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/To_the_Stars), it has continued for twenty years.
