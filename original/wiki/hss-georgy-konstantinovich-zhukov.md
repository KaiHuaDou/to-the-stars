The **HSS Georgy Konstantinovich Zhukov** is the [Battlecruiser](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Battlecruiser) serving as flagship to [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami).

## AI

Georgy Konstantinovich Zhukov looks like an older man with receding hair, clad in an archaic uniform, probably modelling after his namesake, who had a big role in leading the Soviet Army during the 2nd World War through eastern Europe and then conquering Berlin itself.

He affects a Russian accent. Even though his body is a Battlecruiser, he is not beyond playful banter when the situation allows for it. He seems to take some fun from the interactions of his crew.

Interestingly enough, his projected person does not convey the gravitas and power his VR avatar does, easily intimidating [Clarisse](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Clarisse).\[1]

## Physical properties

As a Battlecruiser 'Georgy' is the backbone of any fleet, serving as flagship and mobile command post with his SHERMAN providing powerful backup.
