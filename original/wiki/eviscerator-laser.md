# Eviscerator laser

The Eviscerator laser is a long-range\[1] heavy laser cannon mounted on [Cephalopod](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod) carriers. More powerful than its Human equivalent, the [SHERMAN Cannon](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/SHERMAN_Cannon), it is capable of reducing human [battlecruiser](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Battlecruiser) forcefields by 40% and still mostly penetrating.\[2] It is also used for planetary bombardment.\[3]
