**Eirin the Elusive** was a criminal [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) hunted by the [Soul Guard](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Soul_Guard).

She killed one of [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami)'s protégées by crushing her soul gem.

Implied by her name she may have been responsible for a number of soul crimes to a degree on an unnamed colony the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) was forced to use its influence in [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance) to force a full military intervention.

Her fate remains unknown.
