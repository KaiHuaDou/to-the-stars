# Megane

Luxurious vacation spaceship belonging to [Kuroi Kana](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Kana) and her matriarchy. Used by [Chitose Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chitose_Yuma) to conduct her investigation into a pair of suspicious deep space coordinates.
