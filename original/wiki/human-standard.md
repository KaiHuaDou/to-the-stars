# Human Standard

**Human Standard**, **Standard English**, or simply **Standard** is a [standardized dialect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_language) of English, acting as the *de facto* official language of [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance) and as the largest *lingua franca* for Humanity. Standard was initially engineered a formal middle-ground between English dialects and to supersede the pre-existing informal usage of [English as a Lingua Franca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_as_a_lingua_franca), though it has since become a home language on several Colonies. Pre-existing English dialects continue to exist independently of Standard.

## Known Features

- [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) claims a lack of honorics
- Retains noun inflection for number
