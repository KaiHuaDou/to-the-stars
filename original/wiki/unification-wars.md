# Unification Wars

A series of world wars from 2163 to 2230 between the [Freedom Alliance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Freedom_Alliance) (FA) and the [United Front](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/United_Front) (UF) that devastated Earth and humanity. The war dropped population to less than half the pre-war maximum, and over 200 years later more than 25% of the planet is still uninhabitable as a result.

The [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) eventually succumbed to the economic, political, and magical strengths of the [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF), though the exact role the magic played in the wars is still largely secret. Like the rest of the world, the MSY split in response to the [St. Petersburg Atrocity](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/St._Petersburg_Atrocity), but the vast majority of mages sided with the [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF), including almost all senior leadership.

The wars' name comes from the end result, with the [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF)'s [Emergency Defense Committee](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Emergency_Defense_Committee) (EDC) claiming rule over all of humanity. This would take the form of [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance) just 10 years after the end of the final war.
