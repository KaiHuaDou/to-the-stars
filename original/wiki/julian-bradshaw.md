**Julian Bradshaw** is a prominent [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) historian who has written several popular and scholarly accounts about the organization. He is [Clarisse van Rossum](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Clarisse_van_Rossum)'s most notable protege and sponsored [Trusted Non-Contractee](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/TNC), and often works with her in close concert, as she is both *the* historian of the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) as well as a contemporary witness for large parts of his research.

Due to his academic credentials, as well as his awareness of and integration into the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) prior to the reveal of their existence, he acts as a liaison between [magical girls](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) and much of academia. A series of academic and popular works, including the acclaimed "Mahou Shoujo: Their World, Their History" have made him both popular and trusted enough to carry out his roles. However, being deeply enmeshed with the MSY, his view is that of an insider, with both the strengths and weaknesses that implies.

## Life

As a young man, he was taken in as a trainee by [Clarisse van Rossum](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Clarisse_van_Rossum), in one of her public aliases as a noted historian. In exchange for secrecy, he was later granted Trusted NC status, and spent much of his career working in relative obscurity, with regards to the world outside the MSY. The advent of the [Contact War](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Contact_War) and the revelation of the MSY's existence allowed him to take on a much more prominent role outside the organization.
