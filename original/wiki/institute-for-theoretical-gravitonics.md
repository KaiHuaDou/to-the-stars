# Institute for Theoretical Gravitonics

The lab on Eurydome where [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) and [Asami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Asami) ran wormhole and singularity experiments, under the direction of Director Tao Shaojie. Has a rivalry with the Adept Blue lab. Eccentrically designed to "map meaningfully to a full Calabi-Yau manifold".
