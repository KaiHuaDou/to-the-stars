**Hinata Aina**, also known as **Hinata the Scarlet** and **Hinata the Insane**, was a member of the [Southern Group](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Southern_Group) and as such hunted both demons and fellow magical girls—with only very few exceptions—indiscriminately. Taking manic glee in destruction and violence, she followed a philosophy of "cleansing by fire", though it is questionable in how far this view was merely a justification for her morally skewed actions.

Parts of her psychological background may be attributed to being the survivor of a murder-arson. Nevertheless she started out as a vigilante, starting to kill criminals for increasingly little infringements until she was incorporated into the Southern Group and subsequently reigned in in a manner by the leader of the group, [Mikuni Oriko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mikuni_Oriko). This focused her violent tendencies on [magical girls,](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl) attacking nearly every one on sight.

Hinata was always quick to anger and eager to fight, which leaves the method or arguments employed by Oriko still a mystery.

She fell to an overwhelming force of demons in the fight the Southern Group was factually wiped out in., though the specific circumstances are unknown.

## Costume and Abilities

Colloquially a fire mage, she wore a fitting deeper scarlet costume than [Sakura Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sakura_Kyouko). She had an elemental affinity for fire which allowed her quite interesting feats, such as fire-propelled flight by giving herself "rocket boots" or summoning prodigious amounts of flames.
