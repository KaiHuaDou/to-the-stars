**Ngo Thi An** is a magical girl in the same training cohort as [Shizuki Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko), [Zhou Meiqing](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Zhou_Meiqing) and [Nakihara Asami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Nakihara_Asami). Like her cohort mates, she was en route to Acheron when the transport ship [HSS Laplace](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/HSS_Pierre-Simon_Laplace) came under heavy attack by deep-raiding [Cephalopod](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod) fighters.

In her free time, she plays VR games with some of her cohort mates.

## Costume & Abilities

She can generate an orange barrier that courses with tendrils of light.
