# MSY Founder

**MSY Founder** is a blanket term used to refer to one of the roughly 24 girls who formed the original [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY). As such, they would have been a member of one of the 6 Magical Girl Teams that signed the original [MSY Charter](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY_Charter). In order of signing, the six teams are listed below, along with known members:

![](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/w/images/thumb/d/db/M4.png/350px-M4.png)

The Mitakihara Four, circa 2065.

- **Mitakihara Four**\
  The core founding team, this team contains all the living magical girl members of the original cast, plus [Chitose Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chitose_Yuma) from [Oriko Magica](http://wiki.puella-magi.net/Oriko_Magica). [Miki Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Miki_Sayaka) was a member of this team, but died before the founding. Unlike the other team names, "Mitakihara Four" is still in common usage by the time of the [Contact War](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Contact_War), and it is the only team with a complete member list.

  - [Akemi Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akemi_Homura)
  - [Chitose Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chitose_Yuma)
  - [Sakura Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sakura_Kyouko)
  - [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami)

- **University Area Group**\
  This team was apparently located in the vicinity of Mitakihara University, and may have contained students of the school. They collectively ran a food stand to garner extra funds.
  - [Kuroi Kana](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Kana)

- **Northern Group**
  - [Chiyo Rika](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chiyo_Rika)

- **Financial District Group**\
  This team once had a member captured and killed by [Oriko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Oriko) of the [Southern Group](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Southern_Group).
  - [Tanaka Yui](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tanaka_Yui)

- **Factory Area Group**
  - Takara Chinami

- **West Kazamino Three**
  - [Yasuhiro Rin](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Yasuhiro_Rin)
