# Timeline

## Historical

Mostly from <https://ttshieronym.tumblr.com/post/28295270913/background-timeline-revealed-so-far>

### Pre-MSY

**1815:** Beginnings of [SMC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/SMC) take root at Congress of Vienna

**End of 19th century:** [Clarisse van Rossum](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Clarisse_van_Rossum) is born

**1910s:** [SMC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/SMC) shattered by World War I

**April 15th, 1912:** [Van Rossum](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Van_Rossum) contracts with the sinking of the RMS Titanic

**1940s:** World War II kills off last remnants of the [SMC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/SMC), in England

**1990s-2000s:** Rough year of birth for [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) [Founders](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY_Founder)

**1995-1996:** Rough year of birth for [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami)

**1996-1997:** Rough year of birth for [Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Homura), [Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyouko), and [Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sayaka)

**October 3rd, 1996:** [Madoka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Madoka)'s date of birth in original timeline

**2001-2002:** Rough year of birth for [Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Yuma)

**2007-2008:** Rough year of birth for [Kaname Tatsuya](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kaname_Tatsuya)

**2008-2010:** Rough year of [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami) and [Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyouko)'s contracts

**2011:** [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami) and [Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyouko) reconcile after [Oriko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Oriko)'s attack on [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami)'s apartment

**March 16th, 2011:** Beginning date of [Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Homura)'s loops in original timelines

**2011:** [Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Homura) and [Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sayaka) contract, [Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Homura) receives timeloop memories upon [Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sayaka)'s death

**April 30th, 2011:** [Walpurgisnacht](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Walpurgisnacht) arrives in [Mitakihara](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mitakihara) in original timelines; [Van Rossum](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Van_Rossum) arrives in [Mitakihara](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mitakihara)

**2013-2014:** [Oriko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Oriko) dies, [Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Yuma) joins [Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Homura), [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami), and [Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyouko) as the [Mitakihara Four](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mitakihara_Four)

### Early MSY

**2021:** Founding of [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY)

**2020s:** Rough year of birth for [Shizuki Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Sayaka)

**2031:** Formation of [MSY Leadership Committee](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Leadership_Committee)

**2043:** Formation of [Soul Guard](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Soul_Guard), [Leadership Committee](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Leadership_Committee) given power to form bureaucracy

**2044:** Institutionalization of MSY Judiciary

**2045:** Beginning of corporate expansion throughout pacific rim and parts of East Asia

**2053:** Formation of [MHD](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MHD)

**2055:** [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) takeover of Yakuza

**2059:** First major charter rewrite (excluding early period), formation of MSY Rules Committee, institutionalization of MSY Citizenship, implementation of restrictive Japanese nativist rules regarding membership

**2061:** "Second generation" mages begin appearing in meaningful numbers

### MSY Expansion

**2065:** [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) takeover of Triad, corporate expansion into China; [Kugimiya Ito](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kugimiya_Ito) and [Nakanishi Aiko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Nakanishi_Aiko) reunite

**2067:** Second major charter rewrite, repeal of nativist policies, formalization of expansionist protocol, federalization policies

**2071:** [Akari](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akiyama_Akari) dies, Mami Reformats herself, Korean Intervention

**2085:** Last independent Japanese magical girls absorbed into [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY)

**2068-2126:** Expansion and globalization of [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY), first attempts at AI; copycat [magical girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl) organizations proliferate, including a new [SMC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/SMC), though most are quickly absorbed

**2085:** Rough beginning of MSY-SMC Cold War

**2110:** [SMC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/SMC) agrees to merger in exchange for concessions

**2138:** Last known non-MSY holdout consents to membership

**2135:** [Pretoria Scandal](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Pretoria_Scandal)

**2136:** Theoretical framework for Friendly AI, the [Volokhov Criterion](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Volokhov_Criterion), completed by [Volokhov](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Volokhov) and [Laplace](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Laplace)

**2148:** [Trusted Computing Framework](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Trusted_Computing_Framework), built by early [Volokhov-friendly](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Volokhov-friendly) AIs, lays basis for provably secure computing and AI design; AIs proliferate

**2130s-2150s:** [Kaname Madoka Jr.](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kaname_Madoka_Jr.) born; [Kaname Tatsuya](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kaname_Tatsuya) dies

### Unification Wars

**2130s-2150s:** [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) members reach effective [hyperclass](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Hyperclass) status; future [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA)/[UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF) distinctions start to become apparent, structural unemployment rises to crisis levels

**2150s:** Future [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) nations become increasingly brutal in the crushing of domestic uprisings

**2160:** UN dissolves amidst irreconcilable discord, is replaced by [Freedom Alliance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Freedom_Alliance) (FA) and [United Front](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/United_Front) (UF), neutral nations slowly descend into civil war

**2160-63:** International situation deteriorates, local wars begin to break out, Incubator offer to intervene is refused by the MSY

**January 20th, 2161:** FA-aligned coup begins the Second Civil War in the United States

**2163-2230:** Time period of what would be later known as the [Unification Wars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Unification_Wars)

**2163-2171:** [St. Petersburg Atrocity](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/St._Petersburg_Atrocity) precipitates World War III; [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) membership votes to apply unprecedented punitive measures on its minority [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA)-sympathetic faction, full [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF)/[FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) interfactional war breaks out soon after

**2165** [Volokhov](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Volokhov), "Father of Friendly AI" assassinated.\[1]

**2171-2175:** A partial peace is established after [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF)-aligned governments turn initial defeats into moderate gains, most notably solidifying Europe

**2175-2179:** [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) begins World War IV with largely successful WMD strikes; the unrestricted conflict quickly becomes ruinous for both sides

**2179-2189:** Near civilizational collapse necessitates another partial peace, [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF) and [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) reorganize

**2180:** [UF Emergency Defense Committee (EDC)](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/EDC) founded

**2189-2215:** World War V; WMDs are more readily countered, and heavily augmented human soldiers take center stage

**2200:** [EDC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/EDC) has largely supplanted the remaining UF governments, passes Universal Readiness Decree of 2200 mandating civilian implants

**2200s:** Grotesque [FA Elites](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA_Elites) enter mass deployment; nevertheless, economic fundamentals and [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) disunity begin pointing towards [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF) victory

**2215-2218:** The Treaty of Paris establishes a temporary peace, with substantial gains for the [UF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/UF) and asylum for the remaining [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) underclasses; mass migration precipitates a refugee crisis

**2218-2230:** "The Cleanse"; remaining [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) nations are slowly overrun

**2230:** [EDC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/EDC) declares worldwide Unification Day (though small [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) remnants will persist for years); the population—now heavily augmented and totaling less than half its pre-war height—celebrates

### Golden Age

**2236:** [Kuroi Abe](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Abe) born.

**2252** Last wanted [FA](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA) magical girls hunted down

**2260-2280:** [First Colonization Wave](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/First_Colonization_Wave) begins the settlement of the [Core Worlds](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Core_Worlds)

**2300-2320:** Second Colonization Wave begins

**2310:** [Kuroi Abe](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Abe) and [his future wife](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko%27s_Grandmother) are on the brink of marriage

**2333-2335:** [Kuroi Nana](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nana) born, with [Kuroi Nakase](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nakase) following shortly after

**2337-2338:** [Erwynmark](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Erwynmark) born on the colony [Bismarck](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Bismarck)

**2346-2348:** [Kuroi Nakase](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nakase) contracts, [Kuroi Nana](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nana) contracts and her wish undoes [Nakase's](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nakase) contract

**2350-2360:** Beginning of [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY) clones project

**2420-2423:** [Azrael](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Azrael) born

**2434-2437:** [Azrael](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Azrael) contracts after the destruction of her colony [Terra Roja](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Terra_Roja)

**2439:** [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami) suggests that the [Mitakihara Four](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mitakihara_Four) take a vacation together

## To the Stars

Chapter numbers cited for events: ⁰ ¹ ² ³ ⁴ ⁵ ⁶ ⁷ ⁸ ⁹

On Earth, time of day translates as follows:

• *Early morning* - Roughly 12am-6am

• *Morning* - Roughly 6am-12pm

• *Afternoon* - Roughly 12pm-6pm

• *Evening* - Roughly 6pm-12am

Elsewhere, time of day is local. (A planet may have more than 24 hours in a day, etc.) Date is Earth-standardized.

### Volume 2

**September 29th, 2460:** Initial raid on the wormhole stabilizer fails.¹⁸ [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko), [Asami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Asami), and [Meiqing](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Meiqing) enter the final memory-suppressed simulation portion of their training.¹⁹

**October 8th:** Now aboard [HSS Zhukov](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/HSS_Zhukov), [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) is awarded a medal by [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami) and informed that she's been assigned to the second wormhole stabilizer mission.²²

**October 9th:** [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) meets some of her MagOps team descending down to Apollo aboard [HSS Raven](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/HSS_Raven), and visits her injured [grandfather](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Abe).²³
