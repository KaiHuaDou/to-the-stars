# Walpurgisnacht

Walpurgisnacht was a witch before [Kaname Madoka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kaname_Madoka) made a wish to rid the universe from them.

It is heavily implied that [Clarisse van Rossum](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Clarisse_van_Rossum) is the [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) that originated her, e.g. in [Chapter 22: Shifting Winds](https://archiveofourown.org/works/777002/chapters/1472621) Clarisse arrives at Mitakihara on the same place and time that Walpurgisnacht did when witches existed.
