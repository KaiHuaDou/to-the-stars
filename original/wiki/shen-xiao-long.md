**Shen Xiao Long** is one of Mami's two assigned bodyguards, the other being [Karina Schei](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Karina_Schei).

Shen is Chinese-born, from Nanjing. She, like both Mami and Karina, has an impressively large chest, especially for her apparent age. When the three of them are together, the effect is enough to draw stares from those not used to their presence.

Between her and Karina, Xiao Long appears to make statements that are more observational in nature.

Xiao Long’s color theme is black. She is implied to be part of the Shen matriarchy.
