# To the Stars

> After centuries of turmoil, a Utopian AI/human government has seized control of Earth, heralding a new age of post-scarcity and space colonization. An unexpected encounter with a hostile, technologically superior alien race has broken the peace, forcing magical girls to come out of hiding to save human civilization. In the midst of it all, Shizuki Ryouko, an ordinary girl, looks to the sky, wondering at her place in the universe.
>
> - —Hieronym, author of To the Stars

This page summarizes the entire story, volume by volume and chapter by chapter, for those who want a refresher on events. It also includes the names of all future volumes and interludes, [as released by](http://ttshieronym.tumblr.com/post/55779437700/future-interlude-title-change) [Hieronym](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Hieronym) many years ago.

Each section is collapsed by default, to avoid unwanted spoilers; use the right-hand "Expand" buttons to view descriptions.

Volume I Summary

> [Shizuki Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko), a regular girl dissatisfied with her utopian life, contracts and becomes a [magical girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl), wishing to leave [Earth](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Earth), explore, and "find my place in this universe". Her life rapidly turns anything but ordinary, as day by day wild events pile up and new secrets from her past reveal themselves. She ends up under the tutelage of both [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami) and [Sakura Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sakura_Kyouko), learns that the demon attack that led to her contract was deliberately caused by someone, discovers that she's the descendant of two powerful competing matriarchies, receives a mysterious vision from a magical girl [Goddess](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Goddess), finds out her parents have been working on a secret project cloning bodies for [magical girls](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl) to use, receives a special V2 [TacComp](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/TacComp), and learns about her [aunt](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nana), protege of [Akemi Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akemi_Homura), whose wish turned [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko)'s own [mother](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nakase) from a [magical girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl) back to a regular human.
>
> All along, [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) struggles to adjust, learning as much about magical and military worlds she's entering as she can. Saying goodbye to friends and family, and starting to make new friends among her fellow [magical girls](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girls), she finally departs [Earth](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Earth), led by her wish out into a strange new universe.
>
> ***
>
> In short: [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) becomes a [magical girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl), and begins to discover her unusual place in the universe.

> [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) goes to school, dissatisfied with her life. She feels out of place in a world where her family at home and friends at school seem to enjoy the near-utopian [Earth](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Earth), even as a [war](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Contact_War) against an [alien](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ceph) menace rages. However, classes are interrupted by the appearance of [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami) nearby, who has taken a detour to check the school for potential [magical girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl) recruits with [Kyubey](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyubey), who claims he has found one. The sighting leaves [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) dwelling on the remote possibility of becoming a [magical girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl), distracting her enough that she unthinkingly agrees to go on a date with another boy from class. [Simona](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Simona), one of her friends, sees that she's unhappy and follows her out to a riverbank with a view of the starport.\
> \
> [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami), on the other hand, surreptitiously visits [Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyouko) in her church to discuss [the Cult](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/The_Cult)'s discovery of irregularities in the grief cube supply chain. She agrees to look into it and departs, only to encounter a demon spawn on her way back. At the riverbank, [Simona](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Simona) is about to confess her feelings for [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko), when they are suddenly attacked by [demons](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Demons). [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) manages to keep the both of them alive for a short while before getting trapped. At the moment she attempts to sacrifice herself to save [Simona](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Simona), [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami) saves them both, and along with [Kyubey](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyubey) explains that [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) has the potential to make a wish and become a [magical girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_girl).
>
> > "I wish I could leave Earth," Ryouko said, looking down, "and explore this world. I want to go where no one else has gone before and find my place in this universe."

[Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Homura) then suddenly heals from her wounds, and her white wings are replaced by huge, horrible black wings. >
