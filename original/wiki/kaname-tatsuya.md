Kaname Tatsuya was the husband of [Atsuko Arisu](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Atsuko_Arisu) and, so, the great-great-grandfather of [Shizuki Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko). He was the brother of [Kaname Madoka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kaname_Madoka) before she became the [Law of Cycles](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Law_of_Cycles).
