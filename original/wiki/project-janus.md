# Project Janus

**Project Janus** was a large scale scientific project issued by the [EDC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/EDC) employing thousands of scientists with a single goal: Developing a means to move [faster than light](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Faster_than_light).

By 2300 they succeeded in this endeavour.

**Motto, Project Janus**: "Humanity cannot live unless it learns to ride the wings of eternity".
