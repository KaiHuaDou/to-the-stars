**Fleet Admiral Karishma Anand** is a member of the [General Staff](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/General_Staff) and currently deployed in the Euphratic Sector under the command of [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami), of whom she is a supporter. On an unrelated note, Anans is on good terms with **General [de Chatillon](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/De_Chatillon)**, whom she calls "Chatil".

She is sharp-nosed.
