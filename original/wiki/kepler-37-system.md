# Kepler-37 System

The **Kepler-37 System** is a human-colonized star system in [To the Stars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/To_the_Stars). A Second Colonization Wave system, it is nonetheless relatively notable, containing strategic assets and a position within blink distance of the Core World Optatum. As such, it becomes the final focus of the alien Euphratic Incursion, and the construction site of a Wormhole Stabilizer.

Appears to be based on the real-world [Kepler-37 System](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler-37).

Known locations within the Kepler-37 system include:

- The colony world Apollo
  - The city of [Heliopolis](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Heliopolis)
- The colony world Artemis
- The gas giant Orpheus:
  - The Kepler-37 Wormhole Stabilizer
- Several unnamed inner rocky worlds and gas giants

In addition, the worlds Apollo and Artemis carry in orbit the prized Gemini Shipyards, a key production facility for the human [Armed Forces](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Armed_Forces).
