# Theological Council

The Theological Council is the governing body of the [Cult of Hope](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cult_of_Hope). The Council holds regular holographic and in-person in the Cult Headquarters in Mitakihara City. There are at least 12 members, of which the following are known:

- [Sakura Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sakura_Kyouko)
- [Tanaka Yui](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tanaka_Yui)
- Mina Montalcini
- [Clarisse van Rossum](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Clarisse_van_Rossum)
- Maria Cortez

The Council appears to decide matters of theological importance to the Cult, as well as interpret significant visions.
