# Gul

A [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) and Admiral in the [Armed Forces](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Armed_Forces).

Labeled an eccentric by her colleague [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami), Gul insists on wearing the magical girl military uniform, despite its less than widespread use among [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl)'s. The uniform being vaguely too large on her teenage body and her green and white cap too large for her head.
