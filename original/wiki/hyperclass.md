# Hyperclass

The economic elites of the 22nd century, raised to unimaginable wealth by that era's advances in automation.

Due to the wealth of the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY), magical girls and their families often enjoyed de facto hyperclass status.

Popular unrest against the hyperclass, and brutal crackdowns by its less benevolent members, eventually lit the fuse of the [Unification Wars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Unification_Wars).
