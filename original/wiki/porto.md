**Marshal Porto** is a Member of the [General Staff](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/General_Staff) and thus among the select few influencing the course of the [military](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Military) in the [Contact War](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Contact_War).\[1]

Aside from that he also played a role in the [Emergency Defense Committee](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Emergency_Defense_Committee) that ended the Unification War and laid the groundwork for [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance).\[2]

He is incidentally among the oldest non-[MG](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MG) humans alive, seeing [Project Eden](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Project_Eden) come to fruition still in his lifetime and thus gaining clinical immortality.\[3]
