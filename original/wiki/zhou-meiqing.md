**Zhou Meiqing** is a [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) and in the same training cohort as [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko) and [Asami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Nakihara_Asami), becoming friends with both.

## Costume and Abilities

Meiqing's abilities are tied to earth and ground, forming and changing shapes, walls and other such earth bending applications. Interestingly enough, there is a gravity tie-in for her power's definition of "ground".

- *"I can manipulate the outer surface of any planetary body with a sufficiently high surface gravity."*

Effectively, she can control the outer surface–within certain limitations–of any body surpassing a surface gravity of 2 cm/s2.
