# Machine for Allocation of Representation

More commonly known as MAR, they are one of the (more notable) surviving Volkohov-friendly AIs who participated in the creation of the [TCF](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/TCF). Many surviving AI designers have been radically updated since that time and it is implied that MAR has been as well (though not said directly). Like many of the other surviving AI designers of the TCF, MAR is housed somewhere in [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance) and prefers to keep a low profile.\[1]

They currently handle the creation and appointment and advise the Directorate on the retirement of both Human and AI Representatives in Governance.\[2]
