# First Colonization Wave

The First Colonization Wave refers to the efforts in colonizing firstly Nova Terra, then Samsara and finally Optatum. Leaving aside the post-Unification Wars efforts to rehabilitate Earth, it brought on the first large scale terraforming projects, so the planets chosen were markedly Earth-like, with pre-existing biospheres and similar physical and chemical conditions. It occurred before the public availability of [IIC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/IIC), and, as such, it resulted in colonial governments much more independent from [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance) than those of the Second Colonization Wave.
