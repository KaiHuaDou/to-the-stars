**Sarah Kaisan** is a [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) currently on demon patrol duty in [Mitakihara](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mitakihara).

## Costume and Abilities

Sarah is clad in blue and white plate armour only lacking a helmet, with a fitting kite shield and Zweihänder taller than herself.

Her power is generating a selectively permeable barrier, which she can also blast outwards.

## Appearance

She's a blonde with hair long enough it can fall in her eyes.
