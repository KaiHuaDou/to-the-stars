# FA Elites

The [Freedom Alliance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Freedom_Alliance)'s "Elites" were cloned soldiers, heavily augmented and controlled by implants, designed for specific military roles. Only some were sentient.

> These soldiers came in different varieties, each shaped by their battlefield role—giant hulks for assault troopers, lithe, giant‐eyed nymphs for snipers. The Tankers were some of the worst, barely more than an out‐sized head on a shrunken body, perfect for connecting directly to the life‐support system of a medium armored vehicle.
>
> - —[Chapter 34](http://www.fanfiction.net/s/7406866/34/To-the-Stars)
