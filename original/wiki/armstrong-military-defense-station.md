# Armstrong Military Defense Station

**Armstrong Military Defense Station** is a military defense station on the near side of the moon, near the southern pole, presumably intended for the defense of Earth. It has a smooth metallic sheen when viewed from Earth and is nearby a blue-green patch of the pole that was once part of a large, suspended terraforming project for the poles. It is one of two known such moon stations, the other being [Aitken Defense Station](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Aitken_Defense_Station), and was once a scientific station.

It is likely it was named after [Neil Armstrong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Armstrong).
