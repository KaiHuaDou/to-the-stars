Feathered‐Defender (A1c654u⊃Feathered‐Defender) is a member of a partner species to Consensus/Ahimsa-extending and has taken a liking to [Ryouko's](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) strange appendages (hair).\[1]

## Apperance

Feathered-Defender is described as being "as tall as Ryouko's chest" and "pattered in black and white" \[2]. It has colorful feathers on it's head, adding height that brings it to approximately Ryouko's face. \[3] and is compared to a penguin visually.

## Function

Feathered-Defender is a partner species to /Ahimsa-extending, existing on the ship while Ryouko was being transported to Experimental Somatic Facility One. Feathered-Defender's presence is part of Consensus/Ahimsa‐extending's ideals to include species that coevolved by "by including them in many of our activities". \[4] Specifically, Feathered-Defender is present to defend Lush-Botantist's inedible plants in the habitat. \[5]
