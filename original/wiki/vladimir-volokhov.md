**Vladimir Volokhov** was an AI researcher in the mid-22nd century. In the wake of the [Pretoria Scandal](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Pretoria_Scandal), and with the help of his artificial research assistant [Laplace](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Laplace) and (without his knowledge) a young girl's wish, he discovered how to mathematically represent and prove the *intentions* of a machine intelligence. His work netted him a Turing Award in 2146, and paved the way for the ubiquity of AI in modern human civilization.

Other works bearing his name include:

- The [Volokhov Criterion](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Volokhov_Criterion) - the requirement that all human Governance Representatives be capable of functioning without their networked enhancements.
- Volokhov Analysis - a means of proving whether a particular machine intelligence is sentient, and whether it has a morality compatible with humanity.

Volokhov was killed in 2165 by an unknown assailant, in the lead-up to the [Unification Wars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Unification_Wars).\[1]
