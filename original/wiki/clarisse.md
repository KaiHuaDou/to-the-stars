**Clarisse** (no other name) is a Version Two [Tactical Computer](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/TacComp), embedded in and around [Shizuki Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko)'s spine. She is one of [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko)'s closest friends and a constant pillar of support, despite deep personal uncertainties about her role in the world and in [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko)'s personal life. Her unexpected sentience threw a wrench into her programmed fate; she was designed to love and support [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko), almost as a mother, completely devoted but firmly in the background. Now, with such a simple and subservient position clearly unethical, she tries to figure out a new one, navigating around [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko)'s relationship with [Asami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Asami), sudden fame as the poster child for Version Twos, and some rather confusing encounters with a magical girl [Goddess](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Goddess). Guided by a wry wit and an insuppressible spirit, Clarisse fights for [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) and for herself, as all the plots swirling around her physical host slowly entangle her too.

One of two TacComps to have witnessed a vision, along with [Machina](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Machina) (as of Chapter 70).

Writer of "Observations on Thinker Civilization".
