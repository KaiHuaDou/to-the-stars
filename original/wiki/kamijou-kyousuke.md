**Kamijou Kyousuke** was a contemporary of the [Mitakihara Four](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mitakihara_Four) as well as [Shizuki Hitomi](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Hitomi).

Considered a prodigy on the violin, an accident left him unable to play in his teenage years. [Miki Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Miki_Sayaka), who was in love with him at the time, used her wish to heal his hands. He was and remained unaware of her feelings till her death.

He later married [Shizuki Hitomi](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Hitomi) and had a daughter with her, [Shizuki Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Sayaka).

His music is still played as of Ryouko's time, perhaps aided by funding from [Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyouko).
