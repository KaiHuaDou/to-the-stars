# Odette François

**Odette François**, the venerable François Matriarch is a member of the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY)'s [Leadership Committee](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Leadership_Committee). She has built a reputation due to her unyielding and vocal paranoia about [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance).

Is the ancestor of [Marianne François](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Marianne_Fran%C3%A7ois) and her daughter Juliet François.
