# D&E Corporation

![](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/w/images/d/db/M4.png)

Stands for "Delivery and Errands". An in-practice example of Homura's idea for leveraging magical powers in business, this corporation soon became one of the early MSY's most successful.

For a while it was publically led by [Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyouko), as "Fukuzawa Anko", and as before that as Fukuzawa Anko's mother.
