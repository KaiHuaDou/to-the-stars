# Telecluster

A colloquial term for a set of magical girl powers loosely related to action at a distance (teleportation, telekinesis, clairvoyance, and mind-reading). The telecluster is notable mainly due to its relevance in *power development*; magical girls have found that, generally speaking, the four powers have a tendency to act as effective gateways to learning the others.
