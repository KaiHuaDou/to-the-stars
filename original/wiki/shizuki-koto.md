**Shizuki Koto** is the paternal grandfather of [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko) and sports the familiar aristocratic cheekbones of his line significantly more than his descendants. For unknown reasons he has fallen out with the Shizuki Matriarchy. His wife is [Kugimiya Hiro](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kugimiya_Hiro).
