# Food

**Food**

Food often appears in friendly or diplomatic gatherings/circumstances. (( Maybe this article should be more general, to include tea? ))

In general, most food is synthesized (at least on Earth). Synthesized food is almost as good as food made manually.

Military implants increase the tastiness of raw meat, after one gets over the dysguesia.

[Sakura Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sakura_Kyouko) generally carries a food item. She also gave half a dozen churros to [Akiyama Akari](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akiyama_Akari), with perfect timing too. Relatedly, suspiciously good interruptions of food service have happened quite a few times.

When [Chitose Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chitose_Yuma) was little, her parents took her to Destiny Land, and then she emerged late afternoon holding a cone of ice cream.

/Ahimsa-extending is very vegetarian, while /Somatic-diversity is more indulgent and does not care about gorging on food.

## Cake

> It had been a nice dream, involving cake.
>
> - —[Shizuki Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko)

**Cake** is a confection that can be made manually from flour, sugar, and other ingredients and baked.

Cake appeared in [Akemi Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akemi_Homura)'s original celebrations of October 3rd and in [Chitose Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Chitose_Yuma)'s 458th (?) birthday.

[Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami) is known for her cooking and in particular strawberry cream cake. Her cake was served to her in her visit to the [Goddess](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Goddess). Incidentally, in Ryouko's visit to [Atsuko Arisu](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Atsuko_Arisu), it was mentioned that strawberry cream cake was her favorite form of snack. This coincidence came up later, when Mami gave Ryouko the Chitose Yuma Medal for Combat Leadership Exceeding Expectations of Age and Training Level (and so this is also Mami's favorite cake).

Interlude II is titled "Madeleines", which is a small rich French tea sponge cake typically baked in a shell-shaped mold.
