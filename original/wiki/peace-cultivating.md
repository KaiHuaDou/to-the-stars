Peace‐cultivating (9Qta3Ba⊃Peace‐cultivating) is a member of Thinker/Ahimsa-extending and has the distinction of being the first Cephalopod to converse with [Shizuki Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko) after her capture at the Pulsar mission.

## Character

Peace-cultivating is, as indicated by his star-name, heavily interested in the end of the war. Like other [Cephalopods](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod), he is a member of "one tentacle" (/Ahimsa-extending) of the greater collective that they operate within. First appearing inside of the Ceph ship that [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko) encounters through the wormhole at the arraigned coordinates, Peace-cultivating rendered medical aide once confirmation of consent was possible, largely through the repair of her Empathy Organ.

## Appearance

Peace-cultivating has metal ornamentation on his body, wearing robes.\[1] The design of the body that is interacted with (as of [Chapter 69](http://www.fanfiction.net/s/7406866/69/To-the-Stars) ) is "suited for water". \[2]
