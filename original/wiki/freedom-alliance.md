# Freedom Alliance

An alliance of countries during the [Unification Wars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Unification_Wars), formed primarily of nations trapped under an unchecked and tyrannical [hyperclass](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Hyperclass), or those forced into alliances with such.

Among its other atrocities, its infamous use of genetic engineering and cloning to create the [FA Elites](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/FA_Elites) soured human culture on cloning for centuries.
