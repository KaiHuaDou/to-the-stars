**General de Chatillon** is the commander of the Nile Sector.

De Chatillon is described as a hard-looking commander. He lashes out at General [Alexander](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Alexander) for suggesting a retreat and appears to favor offensive approaches. The General is unmarried, but [Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mami) notices that he watches Fleet Admiral [Karishma Anand](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Karishma_Anand) in a way that "would have tempted her to speculate"1.

He seems to be an enemy of General Alexander, but also an ally of Mami.
