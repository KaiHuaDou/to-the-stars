**Joanne Valentin** is the german-born Director of Prometheus Research Institute and therefore the direct superior to [Shizuki Kuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Kuma) and [Kuroi Nakase](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kuroi_Nakase).

It is with her permission Kuma's request for installation of the next generation [TacComp](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/TacComp) in his daughter [Shizuki Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Ryouko) was granted.

She is part of the Valentin family, a minor matriarchy with some [Magical Girls](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl), but no real success in climbing up in the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY), to a degree her branch wasn't aware of the MSY's existence. After graduating as a chemist she worked in various labs, without earning much distinction. Disaffected with her work, lacking a social circle she was happy with she started seeing a psychiatrist.

After the war with the [Cephalopods](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod) she claimed to have had an religious experience, prompting her to switch scientific fields, become a physicist and earn a DSc. No more apathetic to her work she distinguished herself by participating in the development of starship force fields, which was rewarded with a Nobel Prize for the principal researches (not including her).

Following that she started researching entropy, the heat death of the universe and magical girls and was the first outsider to realize the implications of the entropy gap in conjunction with magical girls. This in turn put her in the focus of the MSY's Science Division, allowing for her to mingle with their professors and be interviewed and accepted for the position of Managing Director in the Prometheus Research Institute.

She seems to be both a prestigious personality as well as very skilled manager, earning her the Oppenheimer Award.

A diligent and skilled worker, very focused on the task at hand.

- Recipient, Oppenheimer Award for Management of a Scientific Facility Exceeding Upper-end Performance Estimates

## Irregularities

Interestingly enough, some irregularities appeared in connection to Joanne Valentin.

- [Ryouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Ryouko) was sure to recognize her as the person she saw when she was first attacked by demons. There might be some (very loose) connection to the Grief Cube Discrepancy if one takes the supersaturated nature of the grief cubes found close by into consideration.
- She was present just before [Tomoe Mami](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Tomoe_Mami) was about to watch a movie in disguise.
- Based on a gut feeling and the personality of Joanne Valentin, [Yuma](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Yuma) doesn't believe the claimed "religious experience" as the reason for change and suspects a Reformat.

Later ([Chapter 58](http://www.fanfiction.net/s/7406866/58/To-the-Stars)) it would be shown that Joanne Valentin was actually [Akemi Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akemi_Homura), the original Joanne Valentin having a life in the colonies (with consent).\[1]
