**Field Marshal Tsvangirai** is a member of the [General Staff](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/General_Staff) currently commanding forces in the Euphratic Sector and thus the Euphratic Incursion.

In case of an attack he is to lead the defences of the Gemini Shipyards.
