**Shizuki Hitomi** can be considered the ancestor to the Shizuki Matriarchy founded by her daughter [Shizuki Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Shizuki_Sayaka), though Hitomi herself never contracted.

In the previous loop she was best friends with [Kaname Madoka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kaname_Madoka) and [Miki Sayaka](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Miki_Sayaka). Although a contemporary of [Akemi Homura](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Akemi_Homura) neither formed much of a connection with the other.

She married [Kamijou Kyousuke](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kamijou_Kyousuke) with whom she had a daughter, Sayaka, probably named after her childhood friend gone missing.

## Appearance

Many of the influences typical of the Shizuki line derive from her, like the aristocratic cheek bones.
