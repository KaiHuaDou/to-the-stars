# Volokhov-friendly

Adjective describing an [AI](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/AI) which meets the [Volokhov Criterion](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Volokhov_Criterion). Such an [AI](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/AI) is almost certainly, but not necessarily, part of the [Trusted Computing Framework](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Trusted_Computing_Framework).
