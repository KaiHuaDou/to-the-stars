# United Front

An alliance of countries during the [Unification Wars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Unification_Wars), formed primarily of places which still mostly respected the rights of non-[hyperclass](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Hyperclass) humanity.

Formed an [Emergency Defense Committee](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Emergency_Defense_Committee) in 2180 to better coordinate after the devastation of World War IV's nuclear exchanges. By the turn of the 23rd century, the EDC had largely supplanted the individual governments of United Front territories.
