**Risa Flores** is a [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) currently leading a demon patrol group in [Mitakihara](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mitakihara). She is part of the social circle surrounding [Sakura Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Sakura_Kyouko).

## Costume and Abilities

Risa has the ability to read thoughts remotely and wields a throwing axe she not only throws, but also uses telekinetically.

## Appearance

She has curled, long red hair and a freckled face. By the standards of her home country, she would be considered of short stature and small bust.
