**Okamura Yuu** is a [Magical Girl](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) who contracted in 2065. She was a victim of domestic abuse by both her parents. In the wake of her becoming a magical girl she left her family without notice, which lead her mother to inform the police. Inspector [Kugimiya Ito](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kugimiya_Ito) began his investigation while Yuu was safely in a safe house maintained by the [MSY](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MSY). In the wake of the investigation Ito was introduced to the MSY on the condition of Yuu's welfare.
