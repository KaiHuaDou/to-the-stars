# Human

Human beings are one of the three known biological sentient races, the other two being the [Cephalopods](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod) and the [Incubators](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Incubator). All known [Magical Girls](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Magical_Girl) are human, since Incubators don't experience the fluctuations of emotion that power magic and very little is known about Cephalopod society. [Artificial Intelligences](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Artificial_Intelligence) are also sentient and an indispensable part of Human society, but their existence is not biological, rather it's dependent on (large amounts of) computing power.

After the [Unification Wars](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Unification_Wars), most of humankind has been ruled by [Governance](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Governance), though a small part of it isn't. Most notably, those who live in rogue colonies.
