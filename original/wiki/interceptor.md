# Interceptor

A type of light, nimble ship used for attack or support as necessary. Best fielded in large numbers, and used by both [Human](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Human) and [Cephalopod](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cephalopod) [fleets](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Fleets).

## Human

Primary role is fleet defense. They can also support [Mage Corps](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mage_Corps) specifically against attacking interceptors and bombers. However, they have considerably less firepower and survivability than their alien equivalent.

## Cephalopod

Plays a role in both attacking human [MC](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/MC), interceptors, and [frigates](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Frigate), covering for bombers. Also plays a role in fleet defense, especially against MagOps teams. They are often sent out in waves along with bombers, with one such wave being responsible for the ultimate death of [Roland Erwynmark](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Roland_Erwynmark).
