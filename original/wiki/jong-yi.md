# Jong Yi

|Jong Yi||

Notably present at both the Battle of Orpheus and the raid on X-25, Jong Yi has the valuable ability to visually reveal the intentions of others with red lines. In between her two appearances, she gained the ability to reveal past intentions as well, using it to help [Kyouko](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Kyouko) discover the location of [Grigori DeWitt](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Grigori_DeWitt) and the colony's children.
