**Tanaka Yui** is one of the founders of the [Mahou Shoujo Youkai](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Mahou_Shoujo_Youkai) and signed the original MSY charter of 2021 on behalf of the "Financial District Group".

She is a member of the [Theological Council](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Theological_Council) in the [Cult of Hope](https://tts.determinismsucks.net/wiki/Cult_of_Hope) and one of the resident telepaths verifying claims of visions of the Goddess. Peculiarly she covers for lies pertaining to the Goddess and likely connections, since those lies are usually on behalf and request of the Goddess herself.

Her hair is made up in twin tails.
